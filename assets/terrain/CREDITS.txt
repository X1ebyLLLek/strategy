Forests and marshes seen from above (aerial_*.jpg): USGS imagery (The National Map, USGSImageryOnly service,

(offset and cross-fade), large light/dark patches evened out, colour-graded, 1024x1024:
  aerial_broad   — broad-leaved forest, Ozark hills, Missouri (37.092 N, 91.195 W)
  aerial_conifer — coniferous forest, northern Maine (46.05 N, 69.05 W)
  aerial_marsh   — marsh with scrub, Seney, Michigan (46.270 N, 86.054 W)
  aerial_farm_a  — farmland (strip fields, farmsteads), Lancaster County, Pennsylvania (40.030 N, 76.130 W)
  aerial_farm_b  — farmland, Lancaster County, Pennsylvania (40.050 N, 76.105 W)
  (the farmlands: large patches evened out, two copies blended by distance from their own borders, muted)
